1. Extract ALL release files into one folder.
2. Open Steam and sign in.
3. Double-click Start.cmd or SAM.Picker.exe.
4. Check games, then use Check selected, Unlock editable or Lock editable.
5. Review the proposed changes before confirming.

Green: editable candidates. Amber: partly protected. Red: protected.
Gray/unknown: check required or unavailable; retry later.
Protected entries are skipped. Steam can reject saves.
See Last result for each game's outcome.
